# PHASE E — DEFINITIVE RECORD (FROZEN)
### Audit of the CURRENT PRODUCTION STRATEGY

## Headline classification

> **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE OF POSITIVE EXECUTABLE EDGE, WITH POOR ROBUSTNESS TO EXECUTION DEGRADATION.**

At measured baseline: **E[R] = −0.155R, CI95 = [−0.379, +0.103], PF = 0.77.** The confidence interval **includes zero**. The supported conclusion is therefore *"no positive expectancy has been demonstrated"* — **not** *"negative expectancy has been conclusively proven at baseline."*

Separately, and held distinct: under **+1 pip of assumed adverse entry degradation**, the confidence interval excludes zero (E[R] = −0.252R, CI95 [−0.478, −0.009]) and negative expectancy becomes statistically established **under that specific execution assumption**. That stressed result is not generalised into a claim that the baseline strategy's true expectancy is necessarily negative.

## Answers to the ten questions

**1. Was the +11.78p release-time figure free from look-ahead? — NO.** (a) Entry at the M5 candle open *at the release timestamp*, requiring the beat/miss classification to exist before the data producing it — look-ahead by construction, not latency; (b) a mid price, untransactable in principle; (c) the magnitude was itself an artefact of scenario-selection leakage. On the corrected universe with real data, gross expectancy at the release instant is **+0.03p, not +11.78p**.

**2. Does the directional edge remain after removing leakage, harness simplification and co-release contamination?**

**Some statistically supported directional tendencies remain, notably within specific employment-package configurations with strong Wilson lower bounds** — `NFP+Unemployment · beat|inline` (floor 69.7%, n=55) and `miss|inline` (floor 62.3%, n=43), and `Core Inflation+Inflation Rate YoY · miss|inline` (floor 78.0%, n=29). **However, no positive executable trading expectancy has been demonstrated from those directional tendencies using the current production entry logic, Stage C filters, and SL/TP structure.**

These are two separate findings and must not be collapsed:

- **Directional predictability** — evidence found in some package configurations.
- **Executable trading profitability** — not demonstrated under the current production strategy.

The directional information did not disappear. What was not established is that it converts into money through the current implementation.

Two attribution corrections stand: Core Inflation's *single-event* appearance was 96% co-released and does not survive at solo N=1; NFP has **zero** solo observations in the dataset. Both exist legitimately only at package level.

**3. Does the current 5-minute LTA strategy have positive expectancy? — Not demonstrated.** E[R] −0.155R, PF 0.77, −326 pips over 116 trades, max drawdown −457 pips, longest losing streak 13.

**4. At what delay does expectancy cross zero? — No positive window was found at any delay measured** (+0s −2.04p through +5m −2.94p, net of real spread). Per-package: all three majors negative at every delay tested.

**5. Earliest realistically executable manual entry?** The chain (release → data source → app → parse → classify → render → user reads → acts → broker fills) is realistically **10–30+ seconds**. This is moot for the current strategy, since no measured delay showed positive expectancy.

**6. Does positive expectancy survive at realistic entry after spread, slippage and full SL/TP? — Not demonstrated at baseline, and significantly negative under +1p assumed adverse execution.**

**7. Which events/packages remain genuinely tradeable?** **None of the tested events or event packages demonstrated positive expectancy robustly enough to qualify as tradeable under the current production strategy.** Best performers: `NFP·beat|inline` (n=49, PF 0.90, avgR −0.06) and `Core Inflation·miss|inline` (n=16, PF 0.86, avgR −0.12). Of 122 scenarios scanned point-in-time, 13 ever qualified; none met the bar. Negative sample estimates are evidence against the current implementation — they do not prove no configuration could ever possess positive expectancy.

**8. Classification of the qualifying scenarios:**

- **TRADEABLE:** none demonstrated robustly enough
- **RESEARCH ONLY:** `NFP+Unemployment · beat|inline`, `Core Inflation+Inflation YoY · miss|inline` (closest to breakeven, largest samples, strongest directional floors)
- **HISTORICAL LEAN ONLY:** the remaining qualifying packages
- **NO TRADE:** single-event scenarios with ≥80% co-release exposure (`NFP·beat`, `NFP·miss`, `Core Inflation MoM·miss`) — packages misfiled as individual events

**9. Is the 5-minute rule supported? — Unsupported as a systematic rule, but it is not the primary deficit.** The decay curve is flat: ~−2.0p at release vs ~−2.9p at 5 minutes; the wait costs ~0.9 pips. **Do not change it on this evidence** — removing it would not create an edge.

**10. Overall:** **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE OF POSITIVE EXECUTABLE EDGE, WITH POOR ROBUSTNESS TO EXECUTION DEGRADATION.**

**The strategy becomes significantly negative under only 1 pip of assumed adverse execution degradation, demonstrating very little robustness margin.** Outcome flips are strictly one-directional (WIN→LOSS 3/7/10; LOSS→WIN 0/0/0 across 348 simulations).

The golden-trade replay showed approximately a **2.2-pip difference** between the OANDA historical quote and the approximate surviving real entry. That difference included **broker-feed differences, approximate entry-time uncertainty, and potentially execution differences.** It demonstrates strong sensitivity to exact entry price. **It does not establish that the user's real broker systematically produces more than 1 pip of adverse slippage.**

Measured spread at the +5m entry: median 1.85p (p95 2.30p), already embedded in every baseline figure. Assumed stop-slippage cases were reported separately and clearly labelled hypothetical.

## Structural finding — R:R clamp degeneracy

`rr = clip(floor(avg_post_entry/sl*10)/10, 1, 5)` produces a **bimodal, near-informationless** output: **88 of 116 trades forced to exactly 1.0 R:R, 28 to exactly 5.0, effectively nothing meaningful between.** This is a **floor/clamp degeneracy**, not normal event-to-event variation — `max(15, round(avg_heat×1.2))` pins SL at its 15 floor (measured heat 2–10p) and the ratio collapses to a boundary. The app's suggested R:R has carried very little genuine event-specific information. The user's real ISM Services trade at ~2.94R is a structure **the current production formula could not have generated**. Logged as a **Strategy V2 / production research item**, distinct from the other backlog entries. Not changed in this pass.

### B. Current production trading engine

Comprising: current direction logic, Stage C behaviour, the 5-minute workflow, current SL logic, current TP logic, the current R:R clamp, and current execution assumptions.

**Verdict: NOT VALIDATED FOR REAL-MONEY RELIANCE.** There is insufficient evidence of positive executable expectancy; current point estimates are negative; profit factor is below 1; drawdown is material; execution robustness is poor.

The known ISM Services trade demonstrated this difference directly (12p stop at ~2.94R, a structure production could not produce; it won).

There is insufficient historical execution data to make a rigorous profitability verdict on it.

**THE USER'S DISCRETIONARY METHOD REMAINS UNTESTED AND HAS NOT BEEN DISPROVEN.** The automatic signal/outcome/execution logging system is being built specifically to make this question answerable prospectively.
